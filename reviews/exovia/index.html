Exovia

A zero-gravity factory sim replaces conveyor belts with floating layouts, generated asteroids, and pressure-free optimization.

Quick verdict

Factory optimization without the conveyor-belt anxiety.

Exovia changes factory design by removing the floor. Floating production layouts and asteroid maps turn three-dimensional space into the central logistics problem, while the lack of danger and time pressure makes experimentation the point rather than the cost.

What it is

Factories are built in zero gravity around procedurally generated asteroids. Traditional conveyor belts give way to floating production layouts and logistics that use open space.

Asteroid seeds can be shared, and the game explicitly removes time pressure and danger. The challenge is self-directed optimization inside a calmer industrial sandbox.

What stands out

  • Zero-gravity logistics replaces standard ground-based conveyor layouts.
  • Asteroids are procedurally generated and use shareable seeds.
  • There is no time pressure or danger; optimization is intentionally relaxed.

The three-player read

One game. Three save files.

01

The Casual

No failure clock creates room to learn spatial logistics.

Players can rebuild without losing a colony or fighting an attack. Three-dimensional construction still needs excellent camera controls and clear connection previews.

02

The Tactician

Removing belts should create new constraints, not remove them.

The appeal is discovering how zero-G changes throughput and layout. Optimization needs measurable tradeoffs even when the game refuses to punish experimentation.

03

The All-Rounder

A factory game can be a wind-down game.

Shareable seeds and no danger make Exovia easy to revisit without remembering a crisis state. Milestones and visual growth will be important for a sense of progress.

Who this game fits

Best for

  • Factory players who want a calmer optimization sandbox
  • Spatial thinkers interested in true three-dimensional layouts
  • Players who enjoy sharing seeds and comparing solutions

Watch for

  • Camera and placement controls in three dimensions
  • Meaningful constraints without danger or deadlines
  • Progression goals across a long-running factory
