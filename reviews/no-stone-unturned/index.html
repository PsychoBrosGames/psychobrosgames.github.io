Okay, what is this thing?

The larger case is called A Murder Most Fowl, but the investigation extends through three classic cases inspired by fairy tales, jokes, and children's stories. Each feeds into the central whodunnit.

The game advertises more than ten hours of story and a fully voiced cast. British humor is the tone, while the detective structure has to carry the player's attention between punchlines.

Why it escaped the group chat

  • Three smaller cases feed into the larger chicken murder mystery.
  • The game features a fully voice-acted cast.
  • Official materials describe more than ten hours of story content.

The dad tribunal

Three dads enter. Agreement remains unlikely.

01

Dad Who Skipped the Tutorial

Comedy lowers the pressure of getting a clue wrong.

A voiced cast and recognizable story references make the world approachable. Hint design will decide whether the humor stays fun when an investigation stalls.

02

The Descent Guy

The deduction must work without the punchline.

Three linked cases can create satisfying inference if evidence is consistent and conclusions are earned. Comedy cannot excuse arbitrary solutions.

03

Normal-ish Dad

Detective comedy is built for group debate.

Voiced characters and discrete cases make this easy to play with spectators. Ten-plus hours is appealing if each mystery changes the rhythm.

Who should play and what to watch for

Invite these people

  • Groups that solve mysteries together from the couch
  • Fans of British humor and storybook parody

Dad caveats

  • Hint quality when a case stalls
  • Whether solutions remain logically earned
  • Comedy repetition across a longer runtime
